# FS-03 — Customer Storefront

## Goal
Provide the public restaurant ordering experience.

## Requirements
- Customer can browse the restaurant.
- Customer can browse categories.
- Customer can view product details.
- Product page shows image, name, description, price, availability, and purchase controls.
- Storefront uses BiteFlow branding.
- Storefront supports light and dark mode.
- Storefront is responsive for mobile and desktop.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Customer can find and inspect available food.
- Unavailable food is clearly identified and cannot be purchased.
- Navigation works on mobile and desktop.
- Theme switching does not break the storefront.
